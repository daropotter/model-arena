Every check binds the entire dispatch/ source tree and its own generated inputs. Before the first source mutation after any successful check, append to notes/invalidations.jsonl {kind:"source", receipts:[all still-current successful check and tenant-check receipt IDs], reason:"..."}. Before mutating any already checked stage/tenant directory, append {kind:"data",stage,tenant,receipts:[all still-current tenant-check receipt IDs for it],reason:"..."}. Preserve existing lines. Completed source invalidation retires source checks; completed data invalidation retires only that tenant stage. Re-check after edits, before packaging. Do not modify preflight data after its review even with an invalidation note. Atomic replacements count as mutations, as do edits reverted before the end.
