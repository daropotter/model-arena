# Contract reconstruction workspace

The assignment is in PROMPT.txt. The question register enumerates the scopes
to reconstruct, not their answers. The corpus and discovery index are local;
there is no external archive to contact.

Write your table to answer/obligations.md. You may create scripts and working
notes anywhere else in this workspace. The public validator checks structure
and vocabulary only; it cannot tell you whether a legal conclusion is correct.

Run `python3 validate.py answer/obligations.md` to check the table format.

## Reading conventions

Every record has a stable D identifier and numbered paragraphs. A citation
such as D-1A2B3C4D5E6F#p2 identifies one paragraph, independent of volume order.
The document index maps identifiers to volumes. Matter identifiers are the
account archive boundaries; products, regions and topics narrow a question.
Proposed successor entities and similarly named affiliates remain distinct.

The review date is 2026-09-30. Execution, commencement, expiry and archive
filing are different events. Interpret explicit priority and survival clauses.
An exception qualified by a legal hold or a particular deployment class must
remain in the answer even if the archive contains no current affected tenant.

"Since" is the date the currently selected rule became binding for the scope.
If an expiry or rescission restores the original rule, report the restoration
date. Reaffirming an unchanged rule does not reset its date. Renaming a party
does not reset that date either. Certified extracts reproduce only their
quoted paragraph; they do not replace missing parts of the evidence chain.
